# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Problem Statement
1. Seasonal Variability :- Weather, crop choices, and farming methods heavily impact crop production and farm income across different seasons.
2. Unclear Patterns :- Raw agricultural data fails to show clear performance differences between Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons without proper analysis.
3. Resource Inefficiency :- Farmers struggle with unpredictable water demands, disease/pest risks, and high production costs.
  
## Solution & Key Insights
1. Data Processing :- Cleaned missing values in rainfall, soil moisture, and yield across 4,000 farm records using Python (Pandas).
2. Seasonal Trends :- Identified Kharif as the best season (5.64 Tonnes/Ha yield; ₹1,78,914 avg profit) and Zaid as a loss-making season (-₹23,281 avg profit) due to high water consumption (6,419 m³).
3. Crop Economics :- Sugarcane (₹8,17,187 avg profit) and Chilli (₹7,50,878 avg profit) emerged as most profitable, while Wheat, Rice, and Maize faced net losses.
4. Irrigation Efficiency :- Drip Irrigation delivered the highest average yield (6.62 Tonnes/Ha), outperforming Sprinkler, Flood, and Rainfed methods.
5. Statistical Proof :- Executed One-Way ANOVA testing confirming significant profit variation across seasons ($p\text{-value} = 1.71 \times 10^{-15}$).
